About the Project

Campus CleanSpot

Campus CleanSpot is an internal digital facility management platform developed for Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP). It allows students, faculty, and staff to report campus waste, cleanliness concerns, and maintenance faults through a streamlined web interface, ensuring that every issue is formally logged and assigned for resolution by the relevant facilities team.

The platform is administered by the BUP Facilities Management Division. Reports are triaged daily and assigned to the appropriate team — whether Housekeeping, Electrical, Civil Works, or Pest Control. The Issues board provides full transparency to the campus community, showing the current status of every submitted report from the moment it is received to the point of resolution.
